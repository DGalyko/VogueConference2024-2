ON FASHION IN UKRAINE AND UKRAINE IN FASHION

Forces of Fashion returns in a live, immersive format, centering this year's dialogue around Fashion in Ukraine and Ukraine in Fashion.

Ukraine’s fashion identity has evolved into something far larger than a local ecosystem. Today, it operates as a distinct creative force—shaping red carpets, crossing into global pop culture, and challenging traditional definitions of luxury. But how do we turn international attention into lasting cultural and commercial momentum? This year, we bring together visionaries, sales leaders, and creative strategists to explore how local talent navigates global distribution, builds enduring fashion businesses, and defines the future of fashion from within.

The FORCES OF FASHION 2026 conference will cover the following subjects:

Cultural Momentum. From international film sets and sports arenas to iconic red carpets event: a deep dive into how Ukrainian styling, celebrity PR, and creative direction are entering global pop culture—and what it takes to stay there.

When Tech Meets Touch. The future of creative expression—from early access to Spring/Summer 2027 runway analytics to sensory diagnostics and human-centric spatial luxury.

Retail, Sales & Scale. Demystifying commercial growth and international distribution. How forward-thinking brands maintain sales momentum, expand their global footprint, and build lasting audience loyalty.

SPEAKERS

Jean Linda Balke

Founder and Director, Archive of Beauty

A luxury visionary with over 20 years of experience shaping brand worlds, Jean has held creative leadership roles at Swarovski and Rosewood Hotels & Resorts, and founded the fine jewellery label Atelier Nallik. Through Archive of Beauty, she pioneers Sensory Diagnostics—a unique methodology analyzing how light, space, and material culture evoke deep emotional connection. She crafts spatial identity for high-profile clients across Europe, including Swarovski Kristallwelten, boutique luxury hotels, and experiential retail.

Rose Coffey

Senior Strategic Foresight Analyst, The Future Laboratory

Working at the world’s most renowned futures consultancy, Rose specialises in fashion, retail, sensory design and media culture. Her work explores digital identity and the dressed body as a lens for understanding cultural, social and behavioural change. She is regularly featured as an expert in Vogue Business and The Guardian, and she has recently spoken at Berlin Fashion Week.

Vena Brykalin

Editor-in-Chief of Vogue Ukraine

Vena Brykalin was appointed Editor-in-Chief of Vogue Ukraine in May 2023. In this position, he develops the publication's distinctive visual style and enhances the brand's presence on the international stage. Brykalin is included in the BoF 500 ranking of the most influential people in the industry according to the Business of Fashion resource.

Sofiia Duiko

Founder and CEO of Mesonia

An engineer by training and an entrepreneur, Sofiia Duiko has been building her own businesses in the beauty industry for over 10 years. In 2019, she and her husband founded Mesonia, a premium skincare brand distinguished by its science-led approach and innovative formulations. Sofiia is closely involved in every stage of product development, from concept and production to launch in Ukraine and international markets.

Anastasia Ivchenko

Communications Expert, Co-founder of Public Kitchen

Anastasia leads the PR department at Public Kitchen agency, developing communications strategies and high-profile PR campaigns. Their extensive portfolio includes DTEK, H&M, Etnodim, SOVA, Agatha Playing and many others.

Victoria Semerei

Partner and Sales Director at Ruslan Baginskiy

Viktoriia is responsible for the brand’s international development, strategic partnerships and global distribution. With her involvement, Ruslan Baginskiy has expanded its presence in key markets worldwide, establishing partnerships with leading international retailers including Galeries Lafayette, Bloomingdale’s, Nordstrom and Mytheresa. In her work, she combines commercial strategy with a mission to promote Ukrainian culture and craftsmanship around the world.

Alya Gonta

Creative Director, Founder Kvitka.agency

Fashion expert and guest lecturer at Polimoda in Florence, Alya Gonta is a pro at connecting fashion with pop culture. Through her PR agency Kvitka she has put Ukrainian brands, including her own label LESSLESS, onto international film sets—most notably teaming up with the costume designers of The Sex and the City and The Devil Wears Prada 2. From sample production to high-impact celebrity integrations, Alya helps brands turn visibility into cultural momentum.

Violetta Fedorova

Vogue Ukraine chief web editor

Violetta has successfully led the digital editorial team at Vogue Ukraine since 2017, a tenure during which the project's audience has more than tripled. She maintains a special focus on fashion as a business and communication within the industry, and is a recognized contributor to international Vogue titles.

Sense Bank invites you to immerse yourself in a world where the past and the future are combined by the power of art and technology. The conference guests will see how Les Kurbas and Vadim Meller come to life, who virtually "storm" ideas for the future performance, and what the scenery and costumes of the masters would look like in the future. Q&A

Can I purchase a ticket on the day of the event?

You will have the opportunity to purchase a ticket on the day of the event at the price "on the day of the event", subject to availability of tickets.

Can tickets be returned?

Tickets can only be refunded if the event is cancelled.

Will an event occur if an air alert is declared?

If an air alert is issued, the conference will be stopped, and all event participants will be evacuated to a shelter at the event venue.

Where is the nearest shelter?

On the territory of UNIT.City. Before the start of the event, guests will be briefed and shown the evacuation routes to the nearest shelter.

Will there be an online broadcast and access to a video recording of the conference's speeches after its end?

There is no online broadcastand a video recording of the performances.
