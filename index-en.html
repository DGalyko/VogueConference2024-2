ON FASHION IN UKRAINE AND UKRAINE IN FASHION

Forces of Fashion returns in a live, immersive format, centering this year's dialogue around Fashion in Ukraine and Ukraine in Fashion.

Ukraine’s fashion identity has evolved into something far larger than a local ecosystem. Today, it operates as a distinct creative force—shaping red carpets, crossing into global pop culture, and challenging traditional definitions of luxury. But how do we turn international attention into lasting cultural and commercial momentum? This year, we bring together visionaries, sales leaders, and creative strategists to explore how local talent navigates global distribution, builds enduring fashion businesses, and defines the future of fashion from within.

The FORCES OF FASHION 2026 conference will cover the following subjects:

CULTURAL MOMENTUM. From international film sets and sports arenas to iconic red carpets event: a deep dive into how Ukrainian styling, celebrity PR, and creative direction are entering global pop culture—and what it takes to stay there.

WHEN TECH MEETS TOUCH. The future of creative expression—from early access to Spring/Summer 2027 runway analytics to sensory diagnostics and human-centric spatial luxury.

RETAIL, SALES & SCALE. Demystifying commercial growth and international distribution. How forward-thinking brands maintain sales momentum, expand their global footprint, and build lasting audience loyalty.

SPEAKERS

Jean Linda Balke

Founder and Director, Archive of Beauty

A luxury visionary with over 20 years of experience shaping brand worlds, Jean has held creative leadership roles at Swarovski and Rosewood Hotels & Resorts, and founded the fine jewellery label Atelier Nallik. Through Archive of Beauty, she pioneers Sensory Diagnostics—a unique methodology analyzing how light, space, and material culture evoke deep emotional connection. She crafts spatial identity for high-profile clients across Europe, including Swarovski Kristallwelten, boutique luxury hotels, and experiential retail.

Rose Coffey

Senior Strategic Foresight Analyst, The Future Laboratory

Working at the world’s most renowned futures consultancy, Rose specialises in fashion, retail, sensory design and media culture. Her work explores digital identity and the dressed body as a lens for understanding cultural, social and behavioural change. She is regularly featured as an expert in Vogue Business and The Guardian, and she has recently spoken at Berlin Fashion Week.

Vena Brykalin

Editor-in-Chief of Vogue Ukraine

Vena Brykalin was appointed Editor-in-Chief of Vogue Ukraine in May 2023. In this position, he develops the publication's distinctive visual style and enhances the brand's presence on the international stage. Brykalin is included in the BoF 500 ranking of the most influential people in the industry according to the Business of Fashion resource.

Patricia Maeda

Director of Womenswear, Future Snoops

Patricia is a fashion trend forecasting expert specializing in womenswear. She translates emerging cultural shifts, consumer behaviors, and market insights into actionable trend forecasts that inform creative and business strategies. With over a decade of experience spanning trend forecasting and merchandising, and a Master of Arts in Fashion and Cultural Studies from Parsons School of Design, Patricia brings a multidisciplinary, culturally informed perspective to understanding the evolving fashion landscape.

Astrid Boutrot

Women's Buying & Fashion Director, The Webster

Astrid Boutrot has spent the past decade shaping the buying strategy and distinctive assortment at The Webster. Her expertise in balancing established luxury houses with emerging talent has helped define the retailer's unique identity and expand its US footprint. Previously a Wholesale Manager at Givenchy, Astrid brings a global perspective to luxury retail, drawing from her life between Paris and the US.

Maria Zhdanova

Strategic Partnerships Development Manager, Google

Maria leads strategic partnerships with digital publishers in Central Europe and produces a podcast at Google backed by a decade of experience in media, technology, and consulting. A European Eyes & Ears Award winner, she is deeply committed to empowering creative communities.

Sofiia Duiko

Founder and CEO of Mesonia

An engineer by training and an entrepreneur, Sofiia Duiko has been building her own businesses in the beauty industry for over 10 years. In 2019, she and her husband founded Mesonia, a premium skincare brand distinguished by its science-led approach and innovative formulations. Sofiia is closely involved in every stage of product development, from concept and production to launch in Ukraine and international markets.

Anastasia Ivchenko

Communications Expert, Co-founder of Public Kitchen

Anastasia leads the PR department at Public Kitchen agency, developing communications strategies and high-profile PR campaigns. Their extensive portfolio includes DTEK, H&M, Etnodim, SOVA, Agatha Playing and many others.

Alina Frendiy

One of Ukraine’s most prominent influencers, with over 900K followers and a track record of collaborations with global fashion houses such as Dior and Chanel—founded her own clothing and accessories brand, COOSH, in 2017. Since then, the brand has built a retail chain with stores in Kyiv, Lviv, Ternopil, Odesa, and Dnipro. This autumn, Alina took a step toward international expansion by opening a showroom in Warsaw.

Oksana Shevchenko

Oksana has extensive experience in marketing, retail and the fashion industry. In 2024, she joined the TSUM Kyiv team, where she is responsible for developing partnerships with Ukrainian designers and brands.

Through her PR agency Kvitka she has put Ukrainian brands, including her own label LESSLESS, onto international film sets—most notably teaming up with the costume designers of The Sex and the City and The Devil Wears Prada 2. From sample production to high-impact celebrity integrations, Alya helps brands turn visibility into cultural momentum.

Business Strategist, Director of Nomis

For more than 25 years, Lesia Navrotska has worked in business consulting and developed growth strategies for fashion and jewellery brands in Ukraine and internationally. Alyona Ponomarenko

Journalist and editor with 15 years of experience, Alyona Ponomarenko, has a master's degree in law. She left her career in legal to write about beauty, self-acceptance and mental health. She oversees large social project for Ukrainian Vogue and the print beauty supplement. Alyona's texts are constantly featured in international editions of Vogue.

Daria Slobodianyk

Culture editor, Vogue Ukraine

With 19 years of experience in journalism, Daria Slobodianiuk has been the culture editor at Vogue Ukraine since 2015. Together with the team, she has developed a number of international projects celebrating art, fashion and literature. She is the curator of the literary collection Contemporary Ukrainian Prose and Poetry in Vogue Ukraine Edition. Daria’s work is regularly featured in international editions of Vogue and Vanity Fair.

Timur Miroshnychenko

Host
